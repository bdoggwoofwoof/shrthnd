Shrthnd

Learn Gregg, Teeline or Pitman shorthand: strokes, rules, words, short forms and reading.

On drawn outlines the moving red dot shows where the pen starts. The faint horizontal line is the line of writing.
Teeline outlines and the word generator come from teeline.online (MIT). Gregg words are taken from the 1930 Gregg Shorthand Dictionary (Anniversary edition, public domain) via the greggdict scans; Gregg strokes and brief forms were checked against Grascii (MIT). Pitman strokes were checked against pitman-translator (MIT) and the rules on Long Live Pitman's Shorthand; Pitman words are drawn by Steno Pad from New Era rules.
